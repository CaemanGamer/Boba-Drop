Genshin Main: Kinich


How to Build Kinich

Artifacts

Best in Slot: Obsidian Codex

This is because Kinich is a Nightsoul DPS, meaning in combat he will be in Nightsoul Blessing state. A 2-Piece set will increase his damage on-field when in this Nightsoul state and a 4-Piece set will increase his CRIT Rate by 40%. This is a good thing as it allows him to deal CRIT Hits more often.

Artifact Main and Substats

Flower

Main Stat: Default HP%

Substats: CRIT Dmg, CRIT Rate, Atk%, Energy Recharge

Feather

Main Stat: Default Atk%

Substats: CRIT Dmg, CRIT Rate, Atk%, Energy Recharge

Sands

Main Stat: Atk%

Substats: CRIT Dmg, CRIT Rate, Energy Recharge Note that in Sands, Goblet, and Circlet, the stat that is the main stat cannot be the substat

Goblet

Main Stat: Dendro Dmg Bonus

Substats: CRIT Dmg, CRIT Rate, Atk%, Energy Recharge

Circlet

Main Stat: CRIT Dmg

Substats: CRIT Rate, Atk%, Energy Recharge

As apparent in the above list, CRIT Dmg should be your priority, then CRIT Rate, then Atk%. Energy Recharge is not fully required but can still be used.

Weapons

Best in Slot: Fang of the Mountain King

This is Kinich's signature weapon which is a 5-star weapon and may be unobtainable or too high-investment. Kinich's signature is not the best initial investment as it is more beneficial to focus on his C1 and C2 before considering the weapon.

Alternative Weapons include:

A Thousand Blazing Suns. However, This is the 5-Star Signature weapon of the character Mavuika and is as hard to get as Kinich's signature as signature weapons are not always available and requires you to pull on a specific banner. This is only a viable option if you have this weapon already and are trying to find a use for it.

Wolf's Gravestone. This is a 5-Star weapon as well, but it is obtainable from the Standard Banner and is a viable option if you have it or lost a 50/50 on the weapon banner to it.

Earth Shaker. This is a Natlan craftable 4-Star weapon. To obtain, just talk to the blacksmith in the Natlan Stadium and buy the forging recipe. It is best at R5 and is a fine F2P option

Tidal Shadow. This is a Fontaine craftable 4-Star weapon. To obtain, just talk to the blacksmith in Fontaine and buy the forging recipe. You will need a healer for this weapon.

Talents

First Priority: Elemental Skill

Kinich's main source of damage is through his elemental skill. In battle, it locks onto a single opponent. Pressing normal attack will cause him to spin around and charge up his Nightsoul points. When his Nightsoul bar is full, press E to fire a cannon shot. Repeat until duration is over. His Nightsoul points will increase faster when the target is Burning (Pyro and Dendro is applied to the opponent), so Kinich can only be used on teams with only Pyro and Dendro characters. Exceptions can exist if the characters do not apply their respective elements, such as Iansan and Pneuma Furina.

Second Priority: Elemental Burst

Pretty much the only talent you should focus on is elemental skill, but when that is high enough, the elemental burst can be leveled. This burst will release Ajaw on-field who will deal Dendro damage periodically.

Third Priority: Normal Attack

Seriously, don't level this unless you have the spare materials and just want to flex that you can level up every single one of his talents. That's what I did :P

Teammates

Best in Slots

Durin (5-Star)

Durin is an amazing Pyro applicator and res-shredder who is Kinich's best teammate due to his ability to apply Pyro while off-field and lower the resistance of all elements reacting with the Pyro (aside from Cryo and Hydro). He can be on many sets, such as Nobless Oblige, Celestial Gift, A Day Carved from Rising Winds (Though this is usually a DPS set and is less in consideration for a Kinich Team), and Deepwood Memories

Nicole (5-Star)

Nicole isa universal attack buffer which benefits Kinich who scales off of Atk%. Additionally she provides a strong shield which and acts as a sustain. She should be on Celestial Gift Artifact set due to its buffing capabilities in a Hexerei team, although this works best only in teams with another Hexerei character. She should be on an Atk% weapon as her shield and buffs scales off of attack, but Thrilling Tales of Dragonslayer will work as well due to the high attack it gives to the next character switched into. ON TTDS she should be last in the rotation right before switching to Kinich.

Other Teammate Options

Mavuika (5-Star)

Though she is usually used as a separate DPS, she can be used as a Kinich support/sub-DPS due to her off-field pyro application. You are better off using her as a DPS since her kit scales off of her own personal damage instead of buffing others.

Iansan (4-Star)

Though she is most optimal at C6, she is still useful before then. She provides healing and an attack buff to the on-field character after using her ult, but only when the character is moving a lot. Due to Kinich's playstyle, this is possible albeit not consistent since Kinich stops moving every time he fires a cannon shot. She should be on an ER weapon and the Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City artifact set.

Bennett (4-Star)

Though he is most optimal at C6, he is still good at C1 and C0 if you don't have any further constellations. Bennett's ult provides a circle where in its area the active character is healed when lower than 70% HP and is given an attack buff. However, this does restrict Kinich's movement as to make full use of the buffs he needs to remain in the circle. He should be on Noblesse Oblige and have a weapon with a high base attack since that is what is buffs scale of off.

Investments

This refers to high investments such as 5-star limited characters or signature weapons. These use the limited wishes instead

Constellations and Signature

In general, after obtaining Kinich, the best investment for him (not including teammates which are separate characters) is his C1. His C1 in battle will increase the CRIT Damage of his cannon shots by 100%. This is VERY useful as CRIT Damage increases the damage of an attack by that percentage more. For example, a regular attack with no CRIT will do 1000 damage. A CRIT Hit with a character with 100% CRIT Damage only will make the damage twice as much.

As for second investment, before even thinking of his weapon, his C2 is most beneficial. This is because the first cannon shot of his skill will decrease the enemy's Dendro Res and make them take more damage.

Playstyle

Step One: Set up the other teammate's skills and ult. For example, a rotation with Nicole (On TTDS), Iansan, and Durin will go: Durin E-Ult-E, Switch to Iansan, Iansan E-Normal Attack-Q, Switch to Nicole, Nicole E, then switch to Kinich.

Step Two: Kinich will ALWAYS be last in the rotation. Use Kinich skill and latch onto the enemy. Then press Normal Attack. Spam Normal Attack until you can use E, then press his E for the cannon shot.

Step Three (Advanced): After the first cannon shot, use Kinich's ult. Using his ult takes up time, so they extend his skill by the Ult animation duration. However, during this time, Kinich can still gain Nightsoul points, so when the animation ends, he will immediately be able to use his cannon shot.

Step Four (Very Advanced): To fire his cannon shots faster and make more out of his skill duration, hold down ee and quickly release it. This will lead to a shorter firing animation. However, this can be tricky to pull off consistently and is able to miss, so unless you are fighting a still, large opponent, just spaming normal attack and E will suffice.
